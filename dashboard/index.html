How the ranking works

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth. The category multiple is the median market-cap / TVL ratio of the tokenized protocols in the same category — each ratio winsorized to [0.005, 5], shrunk toward the global median with a prior weight of 3, clipped to [0.03, 1.5]. Categories with no tokenized peer use the global median. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, dampened (60% weight, clamped to −25%…+50%). Maturity is 4% a year, capped at 3 years: a long-running, large protocol with no token yet is the launch the market has waited longest for. Breadth is the chain count, log-scaled. The multiple is read off the snapshot itself, never a hardcoded list of names.

“Records no token” means DefiLlama records none for the protocol — it is the record, not proof. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are left out. An implied value is this radar’s own claim, never a prediction that a token will launch, or when, or at what price.
