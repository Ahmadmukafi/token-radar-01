Token Launch Radar

Which pre-token protocol is worth farming next, and what to do about it. Ranked by an implied value the model states, not by TVL alone.

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth — calibrated on the tokenized peers in each category. The global median is 0.17×, dragged down by many small tokenized protocols; each category multiple reflects that category’s larger, more comparable peers.

How the ranking works

Category multiple. For each category, the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of its tokenized protocols (winsorized to 0.005–5), shrunk toward the global median of 0.17× with a prior weight of 3, clipped to 0.03–1.5. A category with no tokenized peer falls back to the global median. Risk Curators read 0.87× on a single peer; Lending reads 0.14× on fifteen.

Growth. The 7-day TVL change at 60% weight, clamped to −25%..+50%. A protocol growing fast earns more than a flat one at the same TVL.

Maturity. 4% a year, capped at 3 years, from the day DefiLlama began tracking the protocol. Older protocols have had longer to earn trust.

Breadth. The chain count, log-scaled, capped at ×1.08. A protocol live on many chains is worth a little more than one on a single chain.

The board. Every protocol DefiLlama records no token for (and that is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades). The watchlist is the 40 of them at or above $50M TVL; the board adds every pre-token protocol below the floor, so it is the whole launch universe, not just the floor.

The farm plan. Six steps per protocol, written for its category: how to enter, the core action, how to size it, what keeps it eligible, the weekly rhythm, and what to track. Steps bend with the protocol’s own 7-day change and with watchlist status.
